# Code of Conduct

Studio Inventory should be a friendly, practical, low-pressure project.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome beginners and explain unfamiliar terms.
- Protect other people's privacy and sensitive inventory data.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, spam, or publishing another person's private information are not welcome.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation when needed. Use GitHub's private reporting tools for sensitive conduct concerns; do not post private details in a public issue.
